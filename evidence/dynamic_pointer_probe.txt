Dynamic pointer probe — 2026-09-12

Payload format: [pointer][flag0,cell0]...[flag7,cell7] (17 bytes).

consumed by the epoch. The epoch adds each flag to its paired cell, so exactly

recreates the workspace for the next invocation.
